# Reading a scorecard

For the complete field guide, read `docs/reading-the-scorecard.md` in the Tessera repository.

`pass^k` is what stayed right on every repeat; `mean` is how often the agent succeeded overall, so the gap reveals inconsistency.
Categories show whether trouble comes from agreement, a resolvable conflict, genuine disagreement, or a missing fact.
The appendix points to the exact failed question, attempt, answer, and missing source.
